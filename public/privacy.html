1. Who we are

VERLONIQ ([legal entity name and ABN to be confirmed]) is an AI automation and business systems agency based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. In this policy, "VERLONIQ", "we", "us" and "our" refer to that business.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What personal information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website form, we collect:

  • your full name and business name
  • your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your phone number
  • your industry and approximate team size
  • the service you're interested in and your description of the problem or process you'd like to improve
  • a record that you agreed to this policy, and the time you did so.

If we turn on the optional anti-spam check on the form (Cloudflare Turnstile), Cloudflare also processes technical signals from your browser to confirm a person is submitting it.

When you book a discovery call through our booking page, our scheduling provider collects your name, email address, chosen time, time zone and any notes or answers you add. We receive these details so we can hold the call.

If you become a client (for example, for an AI opportunity assessment, an implementation project or ongoing support), we collect the contact details of the people we work with and the business information needed for the work. Some projects may involve access to your business systems, which can contain personal information about your staff or customers. We handle that information only as needed to deliver the agreed work, under the confidentiality and data-handling terms in our written agreement with you.

When you contact us directly, we keep a record of the emails, calls and meeting notes involved.

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and font provider automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested (see section 4).

The interactive demo on our home page runs entirely in your browser. Text you type into it is not sent to us or anyone else.

We don't ask for sensitive information (such as health information), and we ask you not to include it, or any of your own customers' personal information, in enquiries or booking notes. You can contact us without giving your name, but we'll need a way to reply.

3. How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiry and answer your questions
  • to schedule, prepare for and hold discovery calls
  • to prepare scopes, quotes, assessments and proposals you ask for
  • to deliver and support the services you engage us for
  • to keep records of our communications with you
  • to operate, secure and improve our website
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell personal information. We'll only send you marketing emails if you've asked to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to keep the site secure. If you open our booking page, Cal.com may set its own cookies, as described in its privacy policy. If we add analytics in future, we'll update this policy first.

7. Storage, security and retention

We store enquiry and booking information in our form, scheduling and email accounts. Access is limited to people who need it and protected with strong passwords and multi-factor authentication [confirm MFA is enabled on all accounts]. Information is sent to and from our website using encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

We don't store enquiries in a database on this website. Enquiries are held by Formspree and delivered to our business email. We keep enquiry and booking records for [24 months] after our last contact, unless you become a client. Client records are then kept for as long as needed for the work and our legal and tax record-keeping obligations. After that, we delete or de-identify them. You can ask us to delete your enquiry sooner.

8. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us (see section 10). We'll respond within 30 days and won't charge you for making a request. If we can't give you access, for example because the law doesn't allow it, we'll explain why.

9. Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. We'll acknowledge your complaint promptly and aim to respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
