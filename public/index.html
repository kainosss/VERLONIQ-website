        VERLONIQ FAQ

        Real work.
        Plain English.

        Straight answers about how VERLONIQ works, what the free call involves and how we approach consulting and AI.

        What happens on the free Business Clarity Call?

        We’ll ask how your business runs, what’s frustrating and what you’d like to improve. You’ll leave with an honest view of where to start and a recommended next step, whether that’s consulting, automation or nothing at all. There’s no obligation.

        Do we have to buy an assessment before you automate anything?

        No. If you already know what you want automated, we’ll go straight to a written automation proposal. The Business Improvement Assessment is a separate service for owners who want an independent review first.

        What do we get from a Business Improvement Assessment?

        A fixed-scope review of how your business operates, with findings in plain English, prioritised recommendations and a written action plan you own. It’s useful on its own: you can implement it yourself, with us or with anyone else.

        Do you provide financial, tax or accounting advice?

        No. We provide practical business and operational recommendations based on the information you share. We don’t provide financial product advice, tax, accounting or legal services, and we’re happy to work alongside your accountant or adviser.

        Do we need to replace our current software?

        Usually not. The goal is to improve how work flows through the systems you already use. We check each platform’s capabilities and permissions before proposing an integration.

        Does every automation need AI?

        No. If simple, predictable rules solve the problem more reliably and cheaply, we’ll use those. AI is most useful for interpreting unstructured inputs such as emails, messages and documents.

        Will AI make decisions for our business?

        Not by default. Important communications, pricing, financial changes and unusual situations should be designed with human review and explicit permission.

        Is the demo on this page real AI?

        No, and we’d rather be upfront about that. The interactive demo uses simple rules running in your browser to illustrate the idea. A real project would be designed, tested and secured around your own systems.
