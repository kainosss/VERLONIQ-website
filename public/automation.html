AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS

Workflow automation for trade and service businesses.

Three practical places to start. Each one is designed around your existing tools and the way your team already works, with people reviewing the decisions that matter.

SOLUTION A

AI Customer Enquiry Assistant

The problem: new enquiries land in different places at all hours. Someone has to read each one, work out what it is, chase missing details and remember to reply.

An appropriately configured enquiry assistant reads each new enquiry from your supported channels and does the sorting for you. It works out what the job is, how urgent it sounds and what’s missing, prepares a suitable draft reply and records everything in one place. Your team gets a tidy, prioritised list instead of a messy inbox.

  • Faster, more consistent first responses
  • Urgent jobs surfaced instead of buried
  • Fewer back-and-forth emails to collect basic details
  • One register of every enquiry and where it’s up to

How the workflow runs

  1. Receives enquiries from supported channels, such as your website form or shared inboxAutomatic
  2. Categorises each enquiry by job typeAutomatic
  3. Identifies requests that sound urgent or safety-relatedAutomatic
  4. Extracts key customer details: name, contact, location, jobAutomatic
  5. Flags missing information you need before quotingAutomatic
  6. Prepares a draft reply in your business’s toneStaff review
  7. Records the enquiry in a central registerAutomatic
  8. Notifies the right staff member, faster for urgent jobsAutomatic

What stays with your team: replies to customers, pricing, safety concerns, complaints and anything unusual are reviewed by a person before the customer hears back. Simple acknowledgements (“thanks, we’ve received your enquiry”) can be sent automatically if you choose. Not every enquiry can or should be answered automatically.

SOLUTION B

Automated Quote Follow-Ups

The problem: quotes are sent and then rely on someone’s memory. Follow-ups happen late, inconsistently or not at all.

We build a workflow that keeps an eye on your outstanding quotes and makes sure each one gets a timely, appropriate follow-up. Your team decides the timing and the tone. The system handles the reminders, the drafts and the record-keeping, and tells you when a person needs to step in.

  • Every quote followed up on a consistent schedule
  • Less time spent remembering who to chase
  • A clear record of what was sent and when
  • Overdue quotes raised with the right person

How the workflow runs

  1. Detects outstanding quotes in your compatible quoting or job systemAutomatic
  2. Schedules follow-up reminders at intervals you chooseAutomatic
  3. Prepares a follow-up message for each customerStaff review
  4. Notifies staff when action is requiredAutomatic
  5. Records each follow-up against the quoteAutomatic
  6. Escalates overdue responses to a manager or ownerAutomatic

Depends on your software: what can be automated depends on your quoting or job management software and whether it offers an API, integration or export. We check this during scoping, before you receive a quote. Once you’re comfortable with the wording, routine follow-ups can be approved as templates and sent automatically.

SOLUTION C

Business Systems Integration & Reporting

The problem: your tools don’t talk to each other, so people copy information between them by hand, and reports take hours to pull together.

Business process automation doesn’t have to mean new software. We look at the tools you already rely on (email, calendars, spreadsheets, CRM, job management and accounting software) and connect the compatible ones so information moves once instead of being re-typed. Routine reports can then be produced automatically.

  • Less manual data entry and fewer copy-paste errors
  • Records kept in step across your systems
  • Routine reports without the end-of-week scramble
  • A clearer picture of what’s happening in the business

What we can help with

  1. Connect compatible business toolsScoped
  2. Reduce manual data entry between systemsAutomatic
  3. Synchronise useful records, such as customers and jobsAutomatic
  4. Automate routine reports, such as a weekly enquiry summaryAutomatic
  5. Improve visibility of business activityAutomatic
  6. Reduce repetitive administrative processesAgreed with you

Honest about limits: we don’t promise to connect every platform. Some tools offer limited access, and sometimes the better answer is a scheduled export or a small process change. We confirm what’s possible with your specific software before proposing anything.

AUTOMATION FAQ

Straight answers.

Common questions from business owners considering AI business automation services.

Who is VERLONIQ’s automation service for?

We focus on Australian small businesses, particularly trades and service businesses with roughly 5–30 staff, where enquiries, quotes and admin take up more time than they should. If your business is different, we’re still happy to talk.

Do we have to buy a paid assessment first?

No. After the free Clarity Call you can go straight to a written automation proposal. The Business Improvement Assessment is a separate, optional consulting service.

Will AI reply to our customers on its own?

Only where you choose. By default, replies are prepared as drafts for your team to review. Simple acknowledgements can be sent automatically if you want, but pricing, safety concerns, complaints and unusual requests should always be reviewed by a person.

Which software can you connect?

It depends on your tools. Many common platforms, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and a range of CRM, job management and accounting systems, offer integrations or APIs, but access varies by product and plan. We check your specific software during scoping and only propose what we can confirm. Product names are examples, not partnerships or endorsements.

Do we need to replace our current systems?

Usually not. The aim is to make the tools you already use work better together. If a change would genuinely help, we’ll explain why and leave the decision with you.

How much does it cost?

Automation projects are quoted individually after scoping, based on complexity, integrations and scope. Software subscriptions, API usage and AI processing costs may be billed separately by the providers. Optional ongoing support is priced monthly and discussed at the proposal stage.

Does every automation need AI?

No. If simple, predictable rules solve the problem more reliably and cheaply, we’ll use those. AI is most useful for reading unstructured information like emails and messages.

Is the demo real AI?

No. The interactive demo is a simulation that runs simple rules in your browser to illustrate the idea. It isn’t connected to a live AI model or any customer systems. A real project would be designed, tested and secured around your own tools.
